# KAOS

KAOS is a strategic foresight and prediction-accountability system built to turn noisy future claims into structured, testable intelligence.

The core idea is simple: predictions are everywhere, but accountability is rare. KAOS captures important claims, records who made them and when, checks what later happened, and turns that history into evidence for better foresight.

## Product layers

- **KAOS Signal**: curated intelligence and relevant future-facing claims
- **KAOS Resolved**: past predictions checked against observable outcomes
- **Forecaster Intelligence**: structured history and scoring of predictors
- **KAOS Live**: tracking of still-open predictions
- **Strategic analysis**: patterns, weak signals, consensus, hype and sector-specific insight for consulting use cases

## Business / consulting use case

The system is designed not only as a publishing engine but as a structured intelligence asset. A sector-specific use case around the Future of Sport has been developed to explore how predictions can be organised into taxonomies, databases, rankings and decision-support views for strategic discussions.
